# Pesto

A big bunch of [[Basil]], a handful of pine nuts, one clove of garlic, parmesan and olive oil.

1. Toast the pine nuts until they smell.
2. Pound the garlic with a pinch of salt.
3. Add the basil a little at a time, then the nuts, then the cheese.
4. Loosen with oil until it falls from the spoon.

Freezes well in an ice cube tray.
