# Tomatoes

Two varieties this year: a cherry for snacking and a paste tomato for sauce. They go in the north bed of the [[Garden plan]] once nights stay above 10 °C.

## Care

- Pinch the side shoots every few days.
- Water at the base, never the leaves.
- Feed every two weeks once the first truss sets.

> [!warning] Blight
> Brown patches on the lower leaves: take them off at once and keep the foliage dry.

Grown beside [[Basil]], which is said to help both.
